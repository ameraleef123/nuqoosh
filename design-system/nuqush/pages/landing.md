## Purpose

Convince a first-year student in one scroll that (1) they can have a real site with no experience, (2) it takes ten minutes and no account, (3) the link is theirs forever. One primary CTA: «ابدأ بلا حساب».

## Section order (skill pattern: Scroll-Triggered Storytelling, adapted)

1. **Hero** — wordmark, headline «موقعك الشخصي… من غير خبرة، ومن غير حساب.», sub «عبّي نموذج واحد، واحصل على موقع حيّ برابط دائم. عشر دقايق.», CTA «ابدأ بلا حساب» (primary) + «شوف القوالب» (ghost). Beside it: a live **demo card** = the real `TemplateRenderer` hero for the no-experience fixture, cycling through the 6 free templates with the FLIP crossfade every 6s (pauses on hover/focus, stops under reduced motion).
2. **Chapter 1 — المشكلة:** «سيرتك الذاتية فاضية؟ مش فاضية. مشاريع المساقات هي شغلك.» Three glass cards: مشروع مساق · تطوّع = قيادة · مهارات.
3. **Chapter 2 — الرحلة:** the six steps as a horizontal pill timeline (١ من أنت … ٦ الروابط) with «الخبرة اختيارية» visibly marked.
4. **Chapter 3 — الحل:** gallery teaser — 6 free templates as mood cards (names only, no "Layout 3"), rendered with the fixture, link to `/gallery`.
5. **Trust:** «الرابط إلك للأبد» · «صفحتك عامة، رقمك خاص» · «مجاني فعلًا» — three lines, icons from Lucide, no fake testimonials, no fake counters at launch.
6. **Climax CTA:** «ابدأ بلا حساب» repeated, glass panel over the most vivid mesh on the page.
7. **Footer:** links, language/theme toggles, «الإبلاغ», legal.

## Layout Overrides

- Max width 1200px; hero is a 2-column grid at ≥1024 (copy 6/12, demo card 6/12), stacked below.
- Colour strategy from the skill pattern ("each chapter has a distinct colour, building intensity"): implemented as the **mesh orbs shifting hue per chapter** (lavender → mint → peach → indigo), not as different text colours. Text stays `--color-foreground`.

## Motion Overrides (motion 7/10)

- Hero headline: SplitText **words** (not chars — the headline is > 8 words in Arabic), 500ms, stagger 0.04, `expo.out`.
- Demo card: FLIP between templates (600ms `expo.inOut`); the surrounding copy does not move.
- Chapters: Scroll Reveal subtle preset; the step timeline uses Stagger List subtle (0.03).
- Orbs: 3 layers, `drift` signature, hue shift per chapter via `opacity` crossfade of pre-coloured orbs (never animate `filter`/`hue-rotate`).
- Parallax: **none** (mobile-first; the skill flags parallax as a nausea risk and the brief drops it on mobile).

## Copy tone

Colloquial Jordanian-leaning Arabic, second person, short lines. Never «سجّل الآن», never «أنشئ حسابك». The word «حساب» appears only inside «بلا حساب».
